About me

What I've discovered over the years is that I enjoy most is helping others with their projects and sharing the experience where a project, initially ill-defined, ends up becoming successful. I offer my acquired experience. Helping not only with technical solutions but also with my project management skills."

In each project, I learn from the people who form it as each one brings a unique perspective, and I adapt my skills to offer what is necessary at each moment, whether it is building technical solutions or managing organizational aspects.

I am excited to be part of a project whether it has just begun or has already come a long way. My goal is to contribute to the success of each project by bringing my experience.
